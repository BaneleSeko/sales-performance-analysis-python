# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis using Python

## 🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue, product performance, and sales representatives. The goal is to transform raw data into actionable insights using Python.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales performance across different product categories
* Identify top-performing sales representatives
* Evaluate revenue distribution by region
* Generate insights to support business decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Jupyter Notebook (VS Code)

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains sales transaction records including:

* Product ID
* Sale Date
* Sales Representative
* Region
* Product Category
* Unit Price
* Unit Cost
* Quantity Sold

---

## ⚙️ Data Processing

The following steps were performed:

* Loaded dataset using Pandas
* Cleaned data (removed duplicates, formatted dates)
* Created new features:

  * Revenue = Unit Price × Quantity Sold
  * Cost = Unit Cost × Quantity Sold
  * Profit = Revenue − Cost

---

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 🔹 Revenue by Product Category

* Clothing generated the highest revenue
* Furniture and Electronics followed closely
* Food had the lowest revenue

### 🔹 Revenue by Sales Representative

* Identified top-performing sales reps contributing the most revenue

### 🔹 Revenue by Region

* Analyzed regional performance and distribution of sales

---

## 📈 Visualizations

* Bar chart: Revenue by Sales Representative
* Horizontal bar chart: Sorted performance comparison
* Pie chart: Revenue distribution by region

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* Clothing is the most profitable product category
* Certain sales representatives significantly outperform others
* Some regions underperform and may require strategic attention

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories like Clothing
* Provide training or incentives to improve lower-performing sales reps
* Investigate underperforming regions for growth opportunities

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python can be used to analyze business data and generate meaningful insights that support decision-making.
